Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy provides a general framework for agency-service enquiries. The signed quotation, statement of work or client agreement for a project may contain more specific terms and should be reviewed before payment.

1. Before work begins

Before paying, clients should confirm scope, deliverables, timelines, payment milestones, revision limits, third-party costs and cancellation terms in writing.

2. Cancellation requests

Send a written cancellation request to Disa using the phone or WhatsApp contact listed below. We will review the request against the agreed project scope, work completed, committed third-party costs and the applicable written agreement.

3. Work already completed

Fees for work already performed, approved milestones, non-refundable third-party purchases or costs that have already been committed may not be refundable, subject to the written agreement and applicable law. Any eligible balance will be assessed individually.

4. Delays and client inputs

Timelines can be affected by delayed content, access, approvals or third-party platforms. Such delays do not automatically create a right to a refund; the applicable project agreement and law will govern.

5. Courses and digital products

Purchases made on Disa Digital Study or another third-party product site may be governed by separate checkout terms and policies. Check the relevant official website before payment; this agency policy does not override those terms.

6. How to request a review

Contact us at +91 62616 42944 or WhatsApp +91 96309 58789 and include the project reference, payment date and reason for the request. We will review it and respond within a reasonable time.

7. Applicable rights

This policy does not limit rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
