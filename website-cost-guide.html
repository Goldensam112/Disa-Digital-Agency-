Website planning

What affects website development cost?

Website pricing depends on the work needed, not just the number of pages. A clear brief makes it easier to compare proposals and avoid surprise costs.

1. Website type and scope

A compact business website, a campaign landing page, a product catalogue and a custom web application have different requirements. List the purpose of the website, the audiences it serves and the actions visitors should take.

2. Number and type of pages

Home, about, services, portfolio, contact, product pages, legal pages and blog content each need planning. Unique layouts and complex page interactions take more effort than reusing a shared template.

3. Content and visual design

Decide who will provide copy, product descriptions, photographs, illustrations, videos and brand assets. Copywriting, editing, original photography and custom visual design may affect the scope.

4. Integrations and functionality

Contact forms, WhatsApp inquiry flows, payment gateways, booking systems, CRM integrations, user accounts, search, dashboards and third-party APIs need different levels of implementation and testing. Specify what data should be stored and who can access it.

5. E-commerce and maintenance

Product variants, inventory, shipping, taxes, payments, order notifications and returns add complexity. Also discuss hosting, domain renewals, backups, security updates, content changes and ongoing support after launch.

6. SEO, accessibility and performance

Agree on technical SEO foundations such as unique metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots rules, structured data where appropriate and analytics setup if needed. Mobile responsiveness, accessibility checks and performance optimization should be part of the plan, not an afterthought.

Questions to ask before approving a quote

  • What pages and features are included?
  • Who writes and approves the content?
  • How many revision rounds are included?
  • Who owns the domain, hosting, source files and accounts?
  • What is excluded and billed separately?
  • What are the payment milestones and handover terms?
  • What support is available after launch?
