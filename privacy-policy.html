Privacy Policy

Disa Digital Agency (“Disa”, “we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be handled when you visit disadigital.online, use the enquiry form, contact us, or follow links to third-party services.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you use our enquiry form, you may enter your name, phone number, requested service, approximate budget and project details. You can also contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp.

2. How the enquiry form works

The website form does not submit your information to a Disa website database. When you submit it, your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the details you entered. Review the message before sending it. If you send it, WhatsApp/Meta processes the message under its own terms and privacy policy. Your device, browser, network provider and the third-party service may process technical data as part of this interaction.

3. Why we use information

We may use information you voluntarily send us to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, prepare estimates, provide services, communicate about a project and maintain reasonable business records.

4. Cookies, analytics and embedded services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to third-party websites such as WhatsApp, client websites and Disa Digital Study. Those services may collect technical data according to their own policies. We do not claim to use analytics or advertising cookies unless such tools are actually added to the live website. If analytics, pixels, advertising tags or other tracking tools are added later, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before use.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell enquiry details. Information may be processed by services you choose to use, or disclosed where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, protect rights and security, or comply with applicable law. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through the general enquiry form.

6. Retention and security

We retain messages and business records for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, project, legal obligations or dispute resolution. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please share only information necessary for your enquiry.

7. Your requests

You may contact us to ask about information you have sent directly to Disa, request correction, or ask us to stop non-essential follow-up. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons. Where information is held by WhatsApp or another third party, you may need to contact that provider directly.

8. Children

Our agency services are intended for businesses and people able to make relevant enquiries. Please do not submit a child’s personal information unless you are authorised to do so and it is necessary.

9. External links

Third-party sites are not controlled by Disa. Review their privacy terms before providing information or making payments on those sites.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when updates are published.
