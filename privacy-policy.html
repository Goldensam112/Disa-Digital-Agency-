Clear information about how our website and services work.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Disa Digital Agency (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this website or contact us. We are based in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, India.

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, business requirements and messages. The inquiry form on this website prepares a WhatsApp message; it does not submit form entries to a website database.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect business communications, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information only as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, business records or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about personal information you have shared or request correction/deletion where applicable, subject to legal and operational requirements.
